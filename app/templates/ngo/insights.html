Air Quality Report — NO₂ & SO₂
What is NO₂?

Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂) is a pollutant from vehicle emissions and industrial activity. High levels indicate urban pressure near your mangrove site. Values above 50 µmol/m² are considered elevated and may stress coastal vegetation.

What is SO₂?

Sulphur Dioxide (SO₂) originates from volcanic activity and coal combustion. Elevated SO₂ can acidify coastal waters, harming mangrove root systems. Values above 1 DU (Dobson Units) warrant monitoring.
