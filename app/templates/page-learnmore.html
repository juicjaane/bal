The Crisis Is Now

1
40% Lost Since 1980

India has lost nearly 1,900 km² of mangroves over four decades due to aquaculture, urban expansion, and pollution.

2
Rising Sea Levels Accelerating Loss

Sea level rise of 3.3mm per year is drowning root systems faster than mangroves can adapt, especially in the Sundarbans.

3
Monitoring Gap Slows Response

Most NGOs lack satellite data access. Without real-time monitoring, mangrove loss goes undetected for months — too late for intervention.
